# Sony ST-SA3ES Interface PCB
## Hardware Requirements and Signal Routing Specification
### Revision 3 - Physical Design Baseline

---

# 1. Project Purpose

The PCB shall be installed inline between the Sony ST-SA3ES display/front-panel board and the mainboard/tuner board.

```text
Display / Front-Panel Board
           |
           v
      Interface PCB
           |
           v
   Mainboard / Tuner Board
```

The PCB shall:

- Monitor tuner-control communication generated by IC701.
- Provide the hardware required to emulate the original LC72130 PLL synthesizer.
- Generate the tuner-status signals expected by IC701.
- Preserve the original Sony front-panel controls, rotary encoder, presets and VFD operation.
- Provide pass-through operation for development and troubleshooting.
- Support a second ESP32 dedicated to Internet-radio streaming and digital audio output.
- Provide accessible test points for an oscilloscope and logic analyser.

---

# 2. Target ESP Architecture

## 2.1 ESP32 #1: Interface Controller

The Interface ESP shall handle:

- LC72130 command decoding and future emulation.
- Mainboard control-signal monitoring.
- Virtual tuner-status generation.
- RDS signal monitoring and future emulation.
- Communication with the Music ESP.

## 2.2 ESP32 #2: Music Controller

The Music ESP shall handle:

- Wi-Fi connectivity.
- Internet-radio streaming.
- Audio decoding.
- Station database and stream selection.
- Metadata processing.
- Digital S/PDIF output.

## 2.3 ESP-to-ESP Communication

The PCB shall provide a direct UART connection and a test header:

```text
Interface ESP TX -> Music ESP RX
Interface ESP RX <- Music ESP TX
Common GND
```

The PCB should also reserve optional handshake pins for future use.

---

# 3. Physical Connector Architecture

The PCB shall provide four physical 9-pin connectors:

```text
J1 = CN701 Front-Panel Side
J2 = CN701 Mainboard Side

J3 = CN702 Front-Panel Side
J4 = CN702 Mainboard Side
```

The PCB is inserted inline:

```text
Front panel CN701 -> J1 -> Interface PCB -> J2 -> Mainboard CN701
Front panel CN702 -> J3 -> Interface PCB -> J4 -> Mainboard CN702
```

The connector footprints, pitch, keying and pin numbering must be verified against the original Sony connectors before PCB production.

---

# 4. Target-State Direction Convention

All directions in this document are defined relative to the **Interface ESP** in the final tuner-replacement configuration.

- **INPUT to ESP**: signal originates on the Sony front-panel/controller side and is converted from 5 V logic to 3.3 V logic before entering the Interface ESP.
- **OUTPUT from ESP**: signal is generated by the Interface ESP, converted from 3.3 V logic to 5 V logic, and sent toward IC701 or the Sony mainboard circuitry.
- **Pass-through + tap**: signal remains physically connected from the front-panel connector to the mainboard connector and is also exposed to the PCB or ESP.
- **Straight through**: signal is connected directly between corresponding connector pins and is not connected to ESP circuitry.

---

# 6. Interface ESP32 Pin Assignment

## 6.1 Inputs to Interface ESP

These signals require 5 V to 3.3 V conversion:

```text
GPIO8   = MUTE
GPIO10  = BLN
GPIO15  = CE
GPIO16  = CLK
GPIO17  = DATA
```

## 6.2 Outputs from Interface ESP

These signals require 3.3 V to 5 V conversion:

```text
GPIO3   = AST
GPIO9   = SIG/SI
GPIO18  = D-IN
GPIO46  = ST
GPIO12  = RDS-C
GPIO11  = RDS-D
```

## 6.3 Reserved Interface-ESP Pins

Reserve at least the following for board-level communication and debugging:

```text
UART TX to Music ESP
UART RX from Music ESP
Optional UART handshake
USB/programming connection
Reset/enable access
At least two spare GPIOs
```

The final UART GPIO numbers shall be selected only after confirming the exact ESP32-S3 module or development-board footprint.

---

# 7. Logic-Level Conversion

## 7.1 Sony 5 V to ESP 3.3 V

Signals:

```text
CE
CLK
DATA
MUTE
BLN
```

Use a 5 V-input-tolerant 3.3 V logic buffer or translator. The design may use a suitable 74LVC-family device, provided that:

- The selected device is verified as safe with 5 V inputs while powered from 3.3 V.
- The output level is 3.3 V compatible with the ESP32-S3.
- Direction-control pins are fixed to the correct direction.
- Output-enable pins have defined default states.
- Each IC has local 100 nF decoupling.
- Test points are available on both the Sony side and ESP side where practical.

Do not assume that every generic `74LVC245` variant is automatically appropriate. The exact manufacturer part number and datasheet must be checked before schematic release.

## 7.2 ESP 3.3 V to Sony 5 V

Signals:

```text
D-IN
AST
SIG
ST
RDS-C
RDS-D
```

Use 74AHCT125-family buffers powered from +5 V.

Requirements:

- 3.3 V ESP outputs must be recognised as valid HIGH inputs by the AHCT device.
- Each output shall include a 470 ohm series resistor before the Sony-side connector.
- Output-enable pins shall have defined safe defaults.
- The preferred default during ESP reset is high impedance where the circuit permits it.
- Provide jumper or solder-bridge options to disconnect each emulated output during bring-up.
- Each IC shall have a local 100 nF decoupling capacitor.

At least six up-shift channels are needed:

```text
Channel 1 = D-IN
Channel 2 = AST
Channel 3 = SIG
Channel 4 = ST
Channel 5 = RDS-C
Channel 6 = RDS-D
```

A spare second device footprint may be included for future emulated outputs.

---

# 8. Per-Signal Routing Modes

For development, the board should support three operating states where technically applicable.

## 8.1 Original Sony Pass-Through

```text
Front-panel/controller signal -> Original mainboard/tuner circuit
```

The ESP may monitor the signal without interrupting the original path.

## 8.2 Monitor Mode

```text
Front-panel/controller signal -> Original circuit
                              -> Level shifter -> Interface ESP
```

The original Sony circuit remains active while the ESP records the traffic.

## 8.3 Emulation Mode

```text
Front-panel/controller commands -> Interface ESP
Interface ESP status outputs    -> IC701/mainboard
Original tuner signal source disconnected
```

Use jumpers, solder bridges, analogue switches or zero-ohm resistor options to make the routing explicit and reversible.

---

# 9. Power and High-Voltage Routing

## 9.1 +5 V

The +5 V rail shall:

- Pass directly between the corresponding input and output connector pins.
- Feed the 5 V level-shifter circuits.
- Be available at a labelled test point.
- Include an optional protected tap for auxiliary circuitry.

The Sony +5 V rail must not automatically power both complete ESP32 systems unless the available current and startup behaviour have been verified.

## 9.2 3.3 V

Provide a regulated 3.3 V rail appropriate for the selected PCB architecture.

If ESP32 development boards are used, prevent back-feeding between USB power, the Sony +5 V rail and onboard regulators.

## 9.3 POWER / RESET Path

The `POWER` signal continues to IC901, an LA5667 reset/power-supervision device, including its RESET-related path.

Target treatment:

```text
Connector IN -> direct pass-through -> Connector OUT
                         |
                         +-> high-impedance test point only
```

Do not drive the POWER line from either ESP in Revision 1.

## 9.4 F and -30 V

The VFD filament supply `F` and VFD high-voltage rail `-30V` shall be routed straight through.

Requirements:

- No connection to either ESP.
- No connection to logic-level translators.
- Adequate trace width and spacing.
- Clear silkscreen warnings.
- Keep the -30 V trace away from ESP, UART, RF and audio sections.

---

# 10. RDS Requirements

RDS-C and RDS-D shall:

- Remain available between the original connector endpoints during monitor/pass-through operation.
- Be connected to the Interface ESP through the 5 V to 3.3 V input-conversion stage.
- Have labelled test points.
- Have isolation options for later RDS emulation.

Target GPIOs:

```text
RDS-C = GPIO10
RDS-D = GPIO11
```

Revision 1 treats both RDS lines as inputs to the Interface ESP. Future RDS emulation may require configurable bidirectional routing, so the PCB should reserve a practical modification path or optional driver footprint.

---

# 11. BLN and MUTE Requirements

## 11.1 BLN

BLN is CN702 pin 1 in the current schematic interpretation and originates from IC701/front-panel control logic. BLN controls the Q304 high-blend switch on the mainboard.

Target state:

```text
BLN = INPUT to Interface ESP via 5 V to 3.3 V conversion
```

The physical BLN path should also support pass-through to the original Q304 circuit during development.

Target GPIO:

```text
BLN = GPIO12
```

## 11.2 MUTE

MUTE is treated as a controller command originating from the Sony control side.

Target state:

```text
MUTE = INPUT to Interface ESP via 5 V to 3.3 V conversion
```

Target GPIO:

```text
MUTE = GPIO8
```

Provide pass-through and isolation options until final audio-path behaviour is validated.

---

# 12. Virtual Tuner Feedback Outputs

The Interface ESP shall generate the following tuner-feedback signals toward IC701/mainboard logic:

| Signal | ESP GPIO | Conversion | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| D-IN | GPIO18 | 3.3 V to 5 V | LC72130 serial/status response |
| AST | GPIO3 | 3.3 V to 5 V | Auto-search/tuning status |
| SIG | GPIO9 | 3.3 V to 5 V | Valid station/signal indication |
| ST | GPIO46 | 3.3 V to 5 V | Stereo indication |

Each signal shall have:

- An AHCT output-buffer channel.
- A 470 ohm series resistor.
- A Sony-side test point.
- An optional ESP-side test point.
- A disconnect jumper or solder bridge.
- A defined safe power-up state.

---

# 13. Music ESP and Digital Audio Output

The Music ESP shall remain electrically separate from the Sony control interface except for:

- Common ground.
- UART communication with the Interface ESP.
- Any explicitly designed power connection.

## 13.1 Optical Digital Output

Provide a connector or fitted footprint for an optical S/PDIF transmitter.

Minimum connector signals:

| Pin | Function |
|---:|---|
| 1 | S/PDIF data output |
| 2 | GND |
| 3 | Required transmitter supply, to match selected optical module |

The transmitter supply voltage must be based on the selected TOSLINK transmitter module, rather than assumed universally to be +5 V.

Place the optical output circuitry near the PCB edge and away from the ESP antennas.

---

                              |

---

# 16. PCB Layout Requirements

- Keep both ESP32 antenna regions free of copper, ground pours, connectors and tall components according to the selected module requirements.
- Keep the -30 V and filament paths away from ESP antennas and low-voltage digital signals.
- Place level shifters close to the relevant connector/interface boundary.
- Place 100 nF decoupling capacitors directly beside each logic IC supply pin.
- Use a continuous ground plane where compatible with the ESP antenna keep-out zones.
- Label all four connectors unambiguously as front-panel side or mainboard side.
- Mark pin 1 clearly on silkscreen and assembly layers.
- Label signal direction on silkscreen where space permits.
- Provide mounting holes and board dimensions suitable for installation in the ST-SA3ES chassis.
- Prevent connector reversal through keying, footprint selection or clear mechanical constraints.
- Include revision identification on silkscreen.

---

# 17. Items Requiring Verification Before PCB Release

The following must be confirmed from the Sony service manual and physical hardware before manufacturing:

1. Exact pin number and signal name for all nine pins of CN701.
2. Exact pin number and signal name for all nine pins of CN702.
3. Connector pitch, series, mating orientation and keying.
4. Actual HIGH voltage for each Sony logic signal.
5. Direction and idle level of RDS-C and RDS-D.
6. Idle and active states of D-IN, AST, SIG and ST.
7. Available current on Sony +5 V.
8. Electrical characteristics of F and -30 V pass-through paths.
9. Exact ESP32-S3 module or development-board footprints.
10. Exact optical S/PDIF transmitter module and supply requirement.
11. UART GPIO assignment for both ESP modules.
12. Safe default states for all AHCT output-enable pins.

Do not release the fabrication files until the CN701 and CN702 per-pin tables are verified.

---

# 18. End Goal

The completed PCB shall allow the ST-SA3ES to retain its original:

- Front panel.
- Buttons.
- Rotary tuning control.
- Preset handling.
- Frequency display.
- VFD operation.
- Sony ES user experience.

The Interface ESP shall interpret controller commands and emulate the former tuner subsystem. The Music ESP shall convert the requested virtual frequency or preset into an Internet-radio stream and provide digital audio through optical S/PDIF.
